# WebBites Product Support & FAQ

## Getting Started

### What is WebBites?

WebBites is a bookmarking app that saves any page — article, video, or product — and lets you find it again instantly with search and tags.

### How do I create a bookmark?

Click the **+** button in the toolbar and paste a URL, or use the browser extension to save the page you're on. New bookmarks land in your **Inbox** until you file them.

### Is there a browser extension?

Yes. Install it from your browser's add-on store and sign in once; after that, one click saves the current page with its title and preview image.

## Account & Sign-In

### How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen choose *Forgot password*, enter your email, and follow the link we send you. The link expires after one hour.

### How do I change my email address?

Open **Settings → Account**, edit your email, and confirm it from the message we send to the new address. Your old address keeps working until you confirm.

### How do I delete my account?

Go to **Settings → Account → Delete account**. This permanently removes your bookmarks and cannot be undone. Export your data first if you want a copy.

## Bookmarks

### How do I organize bookmarks into folders?

Drag a bookmark onto a folder in the sidebar, or open it and pick a folder from the **Move to** menu. A bookmark can live in one folder and carry any number of tags.

### How do I delete a bookmark?

Hover the bookmark and click the trash icon, or open it and choose **Delete**. Deleted bookmarks sit in **Trash** for 30 days before they're removed for good.

### Can I share a bookmark with someone?

Open the bookmark and choose **Share** to create a read-only link. Anyone with the link can view it; they can't edit your copy.

## Search & Tags

### How does search work?

Search matches the title, the page text we captured, and your tags. Put a phrase in quotes for an exact match, or type `tag:recipes` to filter by a tag.

### How do I add tags?

Open a bookmark and type in the **Tags** field, or select several bookmarks and choose **Add tag** to tag them all at once.

## Troubleshooting

### Why am I seeing "Unable to save bookmark"?

This usually means the page blocked our reader or the request timed out. Try again in a moment; if it keeps failing, save the URL manually with the **+** button and we'll fetch the preview in the background.

### Why is my preview image missing?

Some sites don't publish a preview image, and others block automated fetches. The bookmark still works — you can add your own image from the bookmark's **Edit** screen.

### The extension isn't saving pages. What should I check?

Make sure you're signed in inside the extension, that the site isn't a restricted page (like a browser settings screen), and that the extension has permission to read the current tab.

## Billing

### How do I upgrade to Pro?

Open **Settings → Plan** and choose **Upgrade**. Pro adds unlimited bookmarks, full-text search history, and shared folders.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to **Settings → Plan → Cancel**. You keep Pro features until the end of the current billing period, then the account returns to the free plan. Your bookmarks are never deleted by cancelling.
