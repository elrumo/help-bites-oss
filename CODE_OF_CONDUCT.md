# Code of conduct

We want HelpBites to be a place where people can ask questions and contribute without being harassed.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome sexual attention, in any form.
- Don't publish other people's private information.
- Assume good faith, and keep feedback specific and actionable.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may ban people who
repeatedly do. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
